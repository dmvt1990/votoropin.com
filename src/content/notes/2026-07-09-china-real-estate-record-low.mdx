China's real home prices just did something they haven't done in 21 years: fall below where they started. The Bank for International Settlements' inflation-adjusted price index for China hit 85.13 (2010=100) in Q1 2026 — the lowest print since the series began in 2005, when it opened at 88.0. Nominal prices are still 14% above 2010. Adjust for inflation, and two decades of gains are gone.

## The decline is still active

This isn't stale data. National Bureau of Statistics figures, reported by Bloomberg on 2026-06-16, show new-build prices fell year-on-year for a 35th straight month in May. Resale prices across 100 cities kept sliding into June, led by second-tier cities, down 8.21%.

Real, it turns out, is a much colder number than nominal.

*What this doesn't show: developer sales are stabilizing, narrowing for a fourth straight month. Volume may bottom before price does.*
