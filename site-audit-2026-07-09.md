## Priority Fix Plan

Most of the 124 findings trace back to about ten root causes. Fixing these at the template/i18n level resolves the majority of findings in one pass each, rather than editing 18 pages individually.

### 1. RU nav has 2 links not in EN nav — appears on **all 9 pages**
Every single RU page's navigation includes "Сканер" (`/scanner/`) and "Дашборд" (`/dashboard/dashboard.html`) with no EN equivalent. This is very likely **intentional** — the MOEX Options Scanner and the generated dashboard are Russian-market tools with no English audience — but it's flagged everywhere because it's a real EN/RU asymmetry. **Recommended action: confirm intentional, then no code change needed** (or add matching EN entries if you do want parity). One shared header/nav component — a single decision fixes all 9 instances.

### 2. Heading hierarchy skips (H1 → H4 → H3 before any H2) — RITIX, RHIX, RDIX detail pages, both locales (6 instances, one flagged 🔴 major on RHIX EN)
All three index detail pages render the chart-panel title as `<h4>` and the constituents-table title as `<h3>`, both appearing before the page's first `<h2>`. This is a shared component in the index detail template (`src/pages/indices/[slug].astro` or its chart/constituents partials) — promote both to `<h2>` once and it fixes all 6.

### 3. RU number/date formatting doesn't follow Russian convention — home, /indices/, RDIX (RU)
Percentages and figures use `.` instead of `,` for decimals (`-26.29%` → `-26,29%`), and thousands separators are inconsistent (`1,685.81` vs `База 1 000`). This is a formatting-function issue, not per-page copy — likely the same `fmt()` helper used across `index.astro` / `indices/index.astro` needs a locale-aware branch.

### 4. Missing non-breaking spaces in RU typography — home, /indices/, /notes/, /contact
Em dashes preceded by plain spaces (risk of an orphaned dash at line start), numbers split from their units (`48 часов`, `1 000`). Systemic across RU templates — worth a single find-and-replace pass (` — ` → ` &nbsp;— ` pattern, or a small template helper) rather than fixing page by page.

### 5. Untranslated "to" in RU date ranges — RITIX, RHIX, RDIX detail pages (RU)
Max-drawdown rows read `"-35.70% (2025-02-25 to 2026-07-06)"` — English "to" left in an all-Russian sentence. Same underlying data-formatting call on all three pages; replace with an em dash or "с ... по ...".

### 6. RHIX card copy diverges from RITIX/RDIX's shared pattern — home, /indices/ (both locales)
RHIX alone uses "Semi-ann." instead of spelled out, "YTD" instead of "Since inception," and "С нач. года" instead of "С начала расчёта" — breaking the parallelism of what's meant to be three identical card layouts. Since these read the same values from `t.home.research.rhix.*` in the i18n files, a handful of string edits in `en.ts`/`ru.ts` fixes both pages at once.

### 7. Disclaimer/blockquote repeated near-verbatim within the same page — RITIX, RHIX, RDIX detail pages, both locales (6 instances)
Each index detail page states its "not investment advice" disclaimer twice: once in a mid-article blockquote, once in the dedicated Disclaimer section further down. Trim the blockquote to a one-line pointer on all three pages/both locales.

### 8. Index enumeration order mismatch — home, /indices/ (both locales)
Copy says "RHIX, RITIX, RDIX" but the cards render in RITIX, RHIX, RDIX order. Either reorder the cards or the copy — same i18n fix covers both pages.

### 9. Trailing-slash inconsistency in nav hrefs — /indices/, /notes/, /cv, /contact (RU)
`/indices` and `/notes` lack the trailing slash the rest of the site (canonical URLs, hreflang) uses consistently. One shared nav component fix.

### 10. One-off content/copy issues (no shared root cause — fix individually, listed in the appendix below)
Highlights, roughly by severity:
- 🔴 **notes-btc (RU):** "усыновление" ("child adoption") used for crypto "adoption" — a mistranslation that reads as unintentionally comic in an otherwise analytical piece.
- CV: two empty `<ul>` bullets for the Citi internship entry (both locales), 8 straight-quote apostrophes that should be typographic, "Ozon Pharmaceutica" → "Ozon Pharmaceuticals" misspelling, "-ize" vs "-ise" spelling inconsistency on RDIX.
- Several EN/RU structural gaps on the homepage specifically: RU hero is missing the Experience/Focus stat blocks EN has, and RU About section is missing its second paragraph — these look like the RU translation fell behind an EN content update.
- CV (both locales): "Mobility — Open to relocation" quick-fact present in EN, missing in RU.
